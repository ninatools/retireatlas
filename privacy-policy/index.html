Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

RetireAtlas provides retirement-planning information and educational calculators. This policy explains what happens when you visit this website.

Information you enter into calculators

The US Retirement Calculator, Australia Retirement Calculator, and UK Pension Tax Calculator are designed to perform calculations in your browser. The pages state that the information entered is not sent to a server by the calculator. Avoid entering sensitive personal information into any online tool.

Server and technical information

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like most websites, the hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and request logs to deliver and protect the service. Please review GitHub’s Privacy Statement for details about its processing.

Data controller and contact

RetireAtlas is an independently operated website. For questions about the handling of personal data or to exercise a privacy right, contact the website publisher at contactretireatlas@gmail.com. The website publisher's legal name and postal contact details should be added here before the site begins processing visitor data for advertising. Please do not email identity documents or other sensitive information unless a secure method has first been agreed.

Purposes and legal bases

Technical information processed to deliver, maintain and protect the website may be handled on the basis of the website operator's legitimate interests in operating a secure and reliable service, subject to the rights and freedoms of visitors. If you contact the website by email, the information you provide is used to respond to your enquiry and manage the correspondence. Where consent is required for optional cookies, analytics or advertising technologies, those technologies should only be used after the required consent has been obtained.

Your privacy rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, visitors in the European Economic Area may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of personal data, restriction of processing, data portability, or to object to processing. Where processing is based on consent, consent may be withdrawn at any time for future processing. To make a request, email contactretireatlas@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. In Portugal, the supervisory authority is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD).

The specific partners and purposes will depend on the advertising settings selected by the site owner. RetireAtlas will identify the applicable advertising partners and provide the relevant privacy information through the consent interface and/or a linked partner list.

When serving personalized ads in these regions, Google requires publishers to use a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF). The presence of this policy text alone does not mean that a CMP has been installed or configured. Non-personalized or limited advertising may have different technical eligibility, but does not remove other applicable consent and disclosure obligations. See Google’s publisher CMP requirements and Google’s CMP setup guidance.

Google explains how it uses information on partner sites in its Partner Sites policy. You can manage Google ad personalization at Google Ads Settings. You can also make choices through the consent interface when it is presented.

For details, see Cloudflare Web Analytics documentation and its privacy policy.
