Portugal

Atlantic coastline, historic towns and varied regional climates.

Portugal offers coastal communities, larger cities and quieter inland areas. The Algarve is known for its sunny climate, while northern Portugal generally has cooler, wetter winters.

Average household expenditure €23,900/year Portugal · 2022/23 survey
Approximate monthly equivalent €1,992/month Annual figure divided by 12
This is a national household average, not a recommended retirement budget or an estimate for one person. Housing, household size and location can make a substantial difference.

☀️ Climate

Southern areas are generally warmer and drier. The north is usually wetter, and inland temperatures can vary considerably by season.

Thailand

Tropical landscapes, regional food cultures and major urban centres.

Thailand has a tropical climate, with regional differences between the north, central plains and southern coasts. Healthcare access, insurance and visa eligibility should be checked for your circumstances.

Average household expenditure ฿22,420/month Thailand · NSO 2025 figure
Approximate annual equivalent ฿269,040/year Monthly figure multiplied by 12
This is average household expenditure, not an individual retirement budget. Costs vary by region, housing, household size and lifestyle.

🌦️ Climate

Hot weather occurs throughout much of the year. Rainfall and seasonal air quality differ by region; northern cities can experience seasonal haze.

Spain

Mediterranean coastlines, historic cities and diverse regional lifestyles.

Spain combines large metropolitan areas, coastal communities and inland historic cities. Climate, housing and access to healthcare vary by region. Check residency and healthcare eligibility before deciding to move.

Average household expenditure €35,101/year Spain · INE 2025 figure
Approximate monthly equivalent €2,925/month Annual figure divided by 12
Spain's published household expenditure measure includes imputed rent for some owner-occupied or provided accommodation. This means the figure is not directly comparable with the Portugal or Thailand figures shown above.

☀️ Climate

Mediterranean coastal areas often have mild winters and hot summers. Inland and southern cities can experience intense summer heat.

How to compare destinations

Before choosing a place, consider your actual monthly budget, housing, healthcare eligibility, insurance, climate preferences, transport, safety, visa rules and tax position. Visit during different seasons if possible.

Important: This page provides general information for early research. It does not rank countries, provide immigration or tax advice, or guarantee healthcare access. Confirm current details with official authorities before making a decision.
